How to buzz in

  1. Type the room code and PIN from the TV, add your name, and tap Join game.
  2. Pick your team. Your name shows up on the TV.
  3. Hands off the keyboard while the button is grey or orange .
  4. When it turns your team color and says BUZZ!, press any key once.
  5. Press too early and your buzzer locks for 2 seconds . Mashing keeps it locked. One clean press wins.
  6. Gold means you were first: shout your answer! Red means the other team got there first.
