How to play

Two teams, one survey, and a race to buzz in first. The host's call is final.

The rules

  1. Face-off. One player from each team goes to their team's buzzer laptop. The host reads a survey question.
  2. Wait for BUZZ! The buzzers show Get ready for a moment, then turn to BUZZ! at a random time. Press one key when you see it.
  3. No early presses. Pressing before BUZZ! locks your buzzer for 2 seconds, and every extra press restarts the lock. Mashing the space bar only locks you out.
  4. First to buzz answers. If the other player gives a higher answer, their team takes the board instead.
  5. Play the board. The team in control takes turns guessing. Every answer on the board adds its points to that team's score right away.
  6. Three strikes. A wrong guess earns an X. After three X's, the other team huddles and gets one guess to steal every point from the round.
  7. Bigger rounds. Round 4 is worth double points, and from round 5 on it's triple.
  8. Winning. The first team to 300 points wins.
  9. Lightning Round. Two players from the same team answer five quick questions against the clock: 20 seconds for the first, 25 for the second, who can't repeat the first player's answers. Together they need 200 points.

House rules

  • Only the player at the buzzer answers during the face-off.
  • No shouting answers when it's the other team's turn.
  • Hands off the keyboard until the screen says BUZZ!
  • The host decides close calls. No arguing with the host.

Using the buzzer laptop

  1. Open the buzzer address shown on the TV (it ends in /buzzer.html).
  2. Type the room code and PIN from the TV, add your name, and tap Join game.
  3. Pick your team. Your name appears on the TV.
