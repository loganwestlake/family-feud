FACE-OFF

Plug in two keyboards. Team 1 slams the LEFT half of their keyboard, Team 2 slams the RIGHT half of theirs. The host runs the board with the mouse (bottom bar) or from a phone. Open Setup to change keys, questions, and Firebase.

⚙ GAME SETUP

Teams

Buzzer Keys

A browser can't tell two USB keyboards apart — it just sees which key was pressed. So each team owns a different set of keys. Default: Team 1 = left half of the keyboard, Team 2 = right half. Want one big key each instead? Click Learn keys, press the key(s) for that team, then click Done.

Survey Questions

Question on the first line, then up to 8 answers as Answer | points. Leave a blank line between questions. Answers are sorted by points automatically.

Firebase (live buzzer log + phone remote)

Optional. Paste the firebaseConfig from your Firebase project (Project settings → Your apps → Web app). It must include databaseURL. The game works fine without Firebase; with it, every buzz is recorded and you can run the show from your phone.
